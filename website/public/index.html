Four courses, four ways to get hit

Each course has its own music, scenery and a hazard borrowed from Mario Kart 64.

Green Hills

Farm fields, cows, barns and the Monty Moles that pop out of the road.

Sunset Speedway

A city highway with traffic: buses and trucks that you have to weave around.

Frosty Peaks

Snowmen to dodge, sheet ice that keeps you sliding, and penguins on the ice.

Dusty Canyon

A desert with a jump ramp and a steam train crossing the road. Wait for it.

Racing that feels right

  • Powerslides with a hop, and a yellow then red mini-turbo if you toggle the stick.
  • Rocket start if you hit the throttle just before the light turns.
  • Slipstream: tuck in behind a rival for a burst of speed.
  • Rubber-banding AI in Easy, Medium and Hard, with 50cc, 100cc, 150cc and a mirrored Extra class.
  • Light, Medium and Heavy karts that really do shove each other differently.
  • Lakitu starts the race, shows your lap and fishes you out of the water.

Pick your mode

Single Race

Any course, 1 to 7 laps, you against seven CPU racers.

Grand Prix

All four courses in a row, 9/6/3/1 points, and a trophy. Finish 5th or worse and you retry.

Time Trial

Race alone against the clock. Your best run comes back as a ghost kart.

Battle

Balloon Battle in three arenas: Big Donut, Block Fort and Skyscraper.

14 items. Zero fairness.

Mushrooms, bananas, green and red shells, triple shells, the blue spiny shell, a star, lightning, a fake item box and a Boo. Hold a banana behind you as a shield, fire shells backwards, or toss bananas ahead.

Every icon is drawn from a few polygons and circles in code.

One game, every desktop

The same code runs on all of them. Each download is a single self-contained binary: no Godot install, no extra files.

Built by an AI agent, in the open

GoKart was written by Agent! running Auto-Pilot on one goal: make a Mario Kart clone. Every track, kart, effect and sound is generated from code, and the repo carries a unit suite of over 14,000 checks. The blog post tells the whole story, including where it got stuck.

GoKart is an independent fan project inspired by Mario Kart 64. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.
